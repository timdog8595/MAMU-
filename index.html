Welcome! We are Bear Baking Co., an elective of THS. Our goal this year is to raise money for new amps for our band, pop-up tents for the school, and a heated cat bed for the school cat, Ben. We plan to raise this money by selling cookie kits, which you can order in the Buy tab above. We have put a lot of time, effort, and planning into this, so we hope you can support us. Thanks!
